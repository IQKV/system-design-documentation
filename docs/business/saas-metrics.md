# SaaS Metrics Overview

This document provides a brief overview of key performance indicators (KPIs) essential for tracking the health and growth of a SaaS platform.

## 1. MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)

**Definition:** The total amount of predictable revenue that a business expects to receive every month.

- **Why it matters:** It is the "heartbeat" of a SaaS business, providing a clear picture of financial stability and growth trends.
- **Calculation:** `Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) * Total Number of Monthly Users`.

## 2. LTV (Lifetime Value)

**Definition:** The total revenue a business can expect from a single customer account throughout their entire relationship with the company.

- **Why it matters:** Helps determine how much can be spent on acquiring a new customer while remaining profitable.
- **Calculation:** `(Average Order Value * Purchase Frequency) * Customer Lifespan` or `ARPU / Churn Rate`.

## 3. CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)

**Definition:** The total cost of acquiring one new customer through a specific channel or campaign.

- **Why it matters:** It measures the efficiency of marketing and sales efforts.
- **Calculation:** `Total Marketing & Sales Costs / Number of New Customers Acquired`.

## 4. ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)

**Definition:** The amount of revenue earned for every dollar spent on advertising.

- **Why it matters:** Evaluates the effectiveness of specific advertising campaigns.
- **Calculation:** `Revenue from Ad Campaign / Cost of Ad Campaign`.

---

### Key Relationships

- **LTV > CPA:** A healthy SaaS business should aim for an LTV that is significantly higher than the CPA (typically a 3:1 ratio is considered good).
- **Payback Period:** The time it takes to earn back the CPA from a customer's MRR.
